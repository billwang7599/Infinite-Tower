# Vision

Status: **direction** (the user's).

## Goal

An open-world RPG to roam with souls-like fights (stamina, committed attacks, dodges with i-frames, telegraphed enemy
attacks, bosses), modelled on AdventureQuest Worlds "but with more immersive combat", in a world shared with other
players. You explore an HD-2D overworld; touching a monster or walking through tall grass pulls you into a small
arena.

## Decided

- "More open world now": one world, starting in the main kingdom (Highmere), with no floors and no realm locks.
- The SAO-style tower of stacked floors (hence the name) is on hold, maybe for good. The tower-climbing platformer
  this started as is kept at the git tag `tower-platformer`.
- Everything else the user has decided is in AGENTS.md's standing decisions.

## Open
