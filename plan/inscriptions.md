# Inscriptions

Status: **an idea, nothing built.** Blessings are a separate thing (plan/lore.md, "The orders"). Rarity and unique
things are in [rarity.md](rarity.md).

## Decided by the user

- An inscription is a permanent passive skill carved into the core. It's how each player gets a build and a
  playstyle of their own.
- Anything from a plain stat boost to a unique passive. The user's examples:
  - a stat boost
  - always on fire
  - dodging or swinging past your stamina, paid for in HP
  - sword damage heals you
- Not blessings: those come from high faith with a god and have no cap.
- How many a core holds is capped by how far the core has grown.
- A ring shows round the core only once an inscription is carved into it, like Saturn's rings. No empty rings.
- Death keeps them.
- The Collegium of Thresholds carves them: a ring is a skill carved into the soul, the way a pad is a skill carved
  into stone (plan/lore.md, "Copied pads").
- Where they come from: super rare drops, crafting (the common ones), quest rewards, and the like. Learning certain
  legendary words may be one way too. Each has a rarity (rarity.md).
- Removing one costs a lot (essence and gold), and then it's gone: to have it again you earn it again. Later, maybe
  a super rare item that keeps a removed inscription.
- Some inscriptions are unique: only one exists in the whole world, so only one player holds it at a time
  (rarity.md, "Unique").

## Drafted by OptChat (for the user to change)

- **An inscription comes as an item, a sigil** ("Sigil of Embers"): dropped, crafted, given as a quest reward or
  traded like anything else. Until it's carved it sits in the bag, so dying loses it like the rest of the bag.
  Carving uses the sigil up. That makes "earn it back" literal: you need another sigil. Built: five sigils, one of
  each tier above common, drop as treasure (`inscription` in `ITEMS`); nothing carves them yet.
- **Slots grow with the core:** one per step, and the first step of each new realm gives two. Yan Early starts
  with one, so a core at Peak Tian holds 19 (15 steps, plus 4 breakthroughs into Sol, Lux, Ling and Tian).
- **The Collegium charges by rarity,** in coins and monster essence, both for carving and (much more) for removing.
- **No doubles:** a core can't hold the same inscription twice.
- **How it shows:** each ring is tinted by its rarity on the core sphere (the portrait's core view); the character
  panel lists them with what they do.
